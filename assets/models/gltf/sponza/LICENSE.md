The Atrium Sponza Palace, Dubrovnik, model was created by Frank Meinl, based
on the original Sponza model by Marko Dabrovic (2002). Morgan McGuire
corrected the model in 2011. PBR textures by Alexandre Pestana
(www.alexandre-pestana.com).

## Changes

The Khronos glTF files were packed into one binary glTF, and the embedded
textures were re-encoded as JPEG at quality 82. The build converts it to the
engine format: textures are re-encoded as BC5 and BC7 and vertices are
reordered by a mesh optimizer.
